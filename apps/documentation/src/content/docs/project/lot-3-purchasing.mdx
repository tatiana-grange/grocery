Delivery lot 3 from the [architecture plan](/grocery/project/architecture-plan).
Full spec, plan, and task list live in `specs/purchasing/`.

## What it delivers

- **Aggregate pre-orders** — staff pick a supplier and every member's still-pending,
  not-yet-aggregated pre-order line for that supplier's products is combined into one draft
  `SupplierOrder`, quantities summed per product. Products that can no longer be ordered are
  skipped and reported.
- **Send** — a draft order is reviewed and marked sent; its lines are then fixed against
  future aggregation runs. A CSV export summarises it for the supplier.
- **Receive** — staff record what actually arrived, line by line. Stock and the weighted
  average cost price update immediately, short / over discrepancies are flagged without
  blocking, each covered pre-order line is marked fulfilled, and the order flips to
  `received` once every line is fully delivered.
- **Stock & cost price reads** — any product's current stock level, weighted average cost
  price, and full movement history, each movement traceable to its reception.
- **Close** — a sent order that will not be fully honored is closed early, without disturbing
  stock or cost already recorded.

## Backend modules

### `apps/api/src/modules/purchasing`

`SupplierOrder` + `SupplierOrderLine` (aggregated demand), `Reception` + `ReceptionLine`
(what arrived). The status machine is `draft → sent → received | closed`; `received` is
automatic, `closed` is staff-triggered, both terminal. `send` and `close` are guarded by a
pure `checkTransition` (current status + optimistic-lock version). Recording a reception runs
in one transaction (`em.transactional`, same pattern as lot 2's checkout): create the
reception rows, append one `StockMovement` per line, mark contributing `OrderLine`s
`fulfilledAt` (first reception of the product wins, never re-touched), then recheck whether
every line is covered. `Reception` / `ReceptionLine` carry `createdAt` only — written once,
never edited; a mistake is corrected with a new reception.

Enums (`supplierOrderStatus`, `discrepancyKind`, `stockMovementReason`) live in the contract
files, never on the entities. Discrepancy is computed at read time: a `unit` line flags on
any non-zero difference, a `weight` line only outside `product.weightTolerancePercent`.

Endpoints: `POST /admin/suppliers/:supplierId/purchasing/aggregate`,
`GET /admin/purchasing/supplier-orders*`, `.../send`, `.../close`, `.../export`,
`.../receptions`.

### `apps/api/src/modules/inventory`

`StockMovement` — the append-only ledger a product's stock level and cost price are derived
from (constitution principle II). Both values funnel through one helper, `deriveStockLevel`
(`SUM(quantity)` and `SUM(quantity * unitCostAmountCents) / SUM(quantity)`), and are never
cached on `Product`. `InventoryService.recordReceipt` takes the caller's `EntityManager` so
its insert joins the reception transaction. A never-received product is a normal state:
`0` / `null` / no movements, not a `404`.

Endpoints: `GET /admin/inventory/stock`, `GET /admin/inventory/products/:productId/stock`.

### `apps/api/src/modules/orders` (extended)

`OrderLine` gains two nullable columns — `supplierOrderLine` (set once, at aggregation time)
and `fulfilledAt` (set once, on the first reception of its product). Both record a one-time
fact about what happened to the line afterward, not an edit to its checkout snapshot, so
neither bumps `updatedAt`. Aggregation's "not yet aggregated" query is a plain
`WHERE supplierOrderLine IS NULL`.

## Frontend

`apps/web-spa/app/features/` — `admin-purchasing` (supplier-orders list, order detail with
Send / Close / Export actions, the line-by-line reception form, and reception history) and
`admin-inventory` (stock list with search, product stock detail with movement history). A
"Aggregate pre-orders" action sits on each supplier row in the catalog. Routes are under
`/admin/purchasing` and `/admin/inventory`.

## Not in lot 3

Inventory counts and adjustments (a later inventory increment), the `distributor` role (lot 4),
and any wallet debit — this lot only ever writes positive `StockMovement` rows.
