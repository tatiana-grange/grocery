Delivery lot 4 from the [architecture plan](/grocery/project/architecture-plan).
Full spec, plan, and task list live in `specs/distribution/`.

## What it delivers

- **Distribution screen** — staff find a member by name or membership number and see, in one
  call, their status, their balance, and every order not yet handed over, each line showing
  what was ordered against what is currently on the shelf. A pre-order line is only handable
  once lot 3 marked it `fulfilledAt`.
- **Handover** — staff correct each line to what actually leaves the table, then validate.
  The order is marked handed over, stock drops, and the member is charged, in one
  transaction. A line set to zero is recorded as offered and declined.
- **Express order** — a member who did not pre-order buys on the spot. Products are found by
  name or barcode; one call creates a real `Order` and hands it over.
- **Member account** — an append-only ledger. Members read their own balance and movements;
  staff read it at the table and record cash, cheques, and bank transfers.
- **Waiting lists** — pre-orders and in-store orders still to hand over, filtered by date.
- **Reversal** — a mistaken handover is undone by a second, reversing handover. The original
  is never written to.
- **`distributor` role** — opens the table and nothing else.

## Backend modules

### `apps/api/src/modules/wallet`

`WalletEntry` — the append-only ledger a member's balance is summed from (constitution
principle II). One signed `amountCents`: negative charges, positive credits. The balance is
`SUM(amountCents)`, summed by the database and never cached on `Member`. `WalletService`
exports `charge` / `credit` taking the caller's `EntityManager`, so they join the handover
transaction — the same shape `InventoryService.recordReceipt` already had.

Two controllers in one file: `StaffWalletController` at `wallet` (`@StaffOnly()`) and
`MemberWalletController` at `me/wallet` (`@MemberScoped()`). The member-facing route takes no
member id at all, which makes "a member cannot read another's account" structural.

### `apps/api/src/modules/distribution`

`Handover` + `HandoverLine`, write-once on the lot 3 `Reception` precedent: `createdAt` only,
never edited. `reversesHandover` is a **forward** self-link on the reversing row, so "has this
been reversed?" is `EXISTS(reversesHandover = :id)` and the original stays untouched.

Every write runs in one `em.transactional` with a **pessimistic lock on the member row**.
That is what makes "the balance never goes negative" hold when two tills validate at once:
the check is a read then a write, and without serialising, both would see the same balance.
Two staffers serving different members never wait on each other.

Order settlement is derived by query — a line counts as settled when an un-reversed handover
covers it, zero included — rather than tracked in a column a reversal would have to un-set.

Endpoints: `GET /distribution/members*`, `POST /distribution/orders/:id/handovers`,
`GET /distribution/products`, `POST /distribution/members/:id/express-orders`,
`GET /distribution/waiting`, `GET|POST /distribution/handovers/:id(/reversal)`.

### `apps/api/src/modules/inventory` (extended)

`StockMovement.quantity` becomes genuinely signed. An outbound movement is valued at the
product's **current weighted average**, which leaves that average unchanged:

```
(N − q·N/Q) / (Q − q) = N/Q
```

So `buildStockLevel` needed no edit and both derivations still sum the same rows. A reversal
puts stock back at the *original* row's unit cost, so the average lands exactly where it was.
`stockSummarySchema.quantityOnHand` stops being `nonnegative()`: stock is allowed below zero,
because at the table the shelf is the source of truth and refusing a real sale over a stale
number is the worse failure.

### `apps/api/src/modules/auth` / `members` (extended)

`USER_ROLES` gains `distributor`, and `StaffOnly()` is `@Roles('distributor', 'admin')` — an
admin passes without holding the role. `ADMIN_USER_ROLES` stays `['admin']`, so a distributor
gains none of the Better Auth admin plugin's powers.

## Frontend

`apps/web-spa/app/features/` — `distribution` (home with member search and the two waiting
lists, the member screen, the handover form, the express order, the receipt with its reversal
action) and `wallet` (the balance panel, the payment form). Routes live under `/distribution`
behind their own `distribution-layout.tsx`, gated on distributor-or-admin. The back office
keeps its `isAdmin` gate untouched and gains a link across.

## Not in lot 4

Online top-up and payment providers (lot 5), participation planning (lot 6), accounting
exports (lot 7), inventory counts and manual adjustments, printed or emailed receipts,
offline operation, and collection by proxy.

**Known gap**: nothing lets staff correct a mistyped payment. The spec carries no requirement
for it; closing it is a fourth `walletEntryReason` and one endpoint, mirroring the handover
reversal. See `specs/distribution/research.md` §12.
