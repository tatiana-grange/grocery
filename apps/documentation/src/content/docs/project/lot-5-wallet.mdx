## Online top-up

An active member opens the account page, taps a quick amount (10, 20 or 50 euros) or types one
between 5 and 200 euros, and is sent to HelloAsso to pay. On return, the page says the payment
is being checked, then that the account was credited, or that it failed or expired. Nothing is
credited until the application has asked HelloAsso for the result.

- The member is credited the amount they asked for. A voluntary contribution added at HelloAsso
  is never added to the balance.
- A payment that is reported twice, or by both the notification and the return page, writes one
  ledger entry. The credit and the "paid" state are written together under a row lock, and a
  unique provider payment id is the safety net behind it.
- A HelloAsso notification is only a nudge. The route reads nothing from it. It asks HelloAsso
  about the newest unfinished top-ups, at most 20 per call, each at most once every 5 seconds.
- An abandoned, failed or expired payment writes no entry. The member can start again.
- A member with a negative balance may top up. Only active members may.

### A top-up paid short

Sometimes the payer pays less than the amount they asked for. Nothing is credited and the
top-up stays `pending`. Admins are told by one e-mail, so someone can follow it up with the
payer and in the HelloAsso back office.

- The mail goes to the admins only, not to the payer. It names the member, the amount asked,
  the amount paid and the top-up id.
- It is sent once per top-up. The top-up carries a time stamp, `underpaidNotifiedAt`, set in the
  same locked step that checks the payment. A later check reads the stamp and sends nothing,
  whether it comes from the return page or from a HelloAsso notification.
- The stamp is set before the mail is sent. If the send fails, it is logged with the top-up id
  and is not retried. If the process dies in between, the mail is lost.
- If the payer later pays the right amount, the top-up is credited normally and no second mail
  goes out.

## Payment correction and negative balance

A payment that staff typed in by mistake can be corrected. On the distribution screen, staff
open the member, find the payment in the history, press "Corriger", give a reason and confirm.
Any distributor or admin may do it. A member never can.

- A correction is one new ledger row with the reason `payment_correction`. Its amount is the
  original amount with the sign flipped. It points at the original through `correctsEntryId`.
  The original row is never touched, so the history shows both.
- Only a payment typed by staff (cash, cheque or transfer) can be corrected, and only once. A
  top-up, a handover, a handover reversal or a correction is refused with `not_a_manual_payment`.
  A second correction is refused with `already_corrected`. A unique index on `correctsEntryId`
  backs that rule when two staff members click at the same moment.
- There is no "undo a correction". If staff correct by mistake, they record the payment again.
- The reason is kept on the correction row, and the member can read it in their own history,
  like every other note.
- The correction never checks the balance. It can leave the member below zero.

### Negative balance

A balance is still the sum of the member's rows. Nothing about "blocked" is stored: a member is
blocked exactly while the sum is below zero. A balance of exactly zero is not blocked. A top-up
or a new payment that brings the sum back to zero or above lifts the block with no other action.

While the balance is below zero:

- The counter refuses a handover or an express order with `insufficient_balance`. The shortfall
  includes what the account owes.
- The online shop refuses adding a line, changing a line and checkout with `negative_balance`.
  Reading the cart and removing a line still work. The member sees one sentence on the cart page
  and on the account page, the checkout button is disabled, and the top-up form stays open.
- Online top-up is never blocked.

One purchase guard in `apps/api/src/modules/wallet/purchase-guard.ts` is the only code that
refuses a purchase for a balance reason. The counter and the online shop both call it.

### E-mails

When a correction leaves the balance below zero, one mail goes to the member and one to each
admin. The member mail says there is a problem on the account and that purchases are paused. It
gives no amount and does not say a correction happened. The admin mail names the member, the
membership number and the balance. The mails go out after the correction has committed. If a
mail fails, the failure is logged and the correction stays. A member or admin with no real
e-mail address (a phone-only account) gets no mail, only a log line.

## Refund

An admin records money that was given back to a member, by cash, cheque or transfer. On the
distribution screen, the admin opens the member and presses "Rembourser", gives the amount, the
method and a reason, and confirms. The member's balance goes down by that amount.

Nothing is sent to HelloAsso. The admin gives the money back by hand and writes it down. The
reason is in [ADR 0001](/grocery/project/decisions/0001-helloasso-and-recorded-refunds).

- Only an admin can record a refund. A distributor and a member are refused. The button is not
  shown to a distributor.
- A refund is one new ledger row with the reason `refund`. It is negative and carries the method,
  the reason as its note and the admin who recorded it. No row is edited.
- The reason is required. The member reads it in their history, like every other note.
- A refund is never larger than the balance. A balance of zero or below refuses every refund. The
  refusal is `refund_exceeds_balance`, and the answer carries the balance and the amount.
- The check and the write share one transaction under a lock on the member, so two refunds at the
  same moment cannot both pass. A payment correction takes no such lock and may land just after
  the check. That is allowed: a correction can take a balance below zero.
- A member who left may be refunded. The refund does not look at the member status.
- There is no undo of a refund, and a refund cannot be corrected. If a refund was recorded by
  mistake, an admin records the money again as a payment and says why in the note.

## Settings

All optional. Without them the app starts, the top-up button is hidden and the routes answer
with the code `payments_disabled`.

| Variable                      | Meaning                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PAYMENT_PROVIDER`            | `helloasso` or `fake`. `fake` is ignored in production.                                                               |
| `HELLOASSO_CLIENT_ID`         | OAuth2 client id                                                                                                      |
| `HELLOASSO_CLIENT_SECRET`     | OAuth2 client secret                                                                                                  |
| `HELLOASSO_ORGANIZATION_SLUG` | the association's slug on HelloAsso                                                                                   |
| `HELLOASSO_API_URL`           | base URL ending in `/v5`. Defaults to the sandbox, so production must name `https://api.helloasso.com/v5` on purpose. |

HelloAsso accepts only https return addresses, so `CLIENTS_WEB_APP_URL` must be https in a real
run. Notifications are signed only for HelloAsso partner accounts, which is one more reason the
application never trusts a notification and always asks for the result.
