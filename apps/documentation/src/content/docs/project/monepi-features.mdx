Source: [wiki.monepi.fr](https://wiki.monepi.fr/doku.php), read on 2026-09-01. This is the
scope reference. Items marked **(multi-site)** are out of scope for a single location.

## Shop and member area

- Online shop: catalogue by category, search (name, barcode), sorting, categories hidden
  when they hold no available product
- Product page: photos, badges (organic, local, vegetarian/vegan), Nutri-score, Nova-score,
  Eco-score, member ratings
- Cart and ordering: pre-order of fresh products (from the producer) versus in-store order
  (from stock), repeat or duplicate a past order
- Express order: buy in-store from current stock, pay on the spot, scan or type product names
- Member account: sign-up (email confirmation), change password (plus QR code), edit personal
  details
- Pay the membership fee online (HelloAsso / Lydia)
- Top up the member account balance (decimal amounts), IBAN/BIC shown
- Planning: sign up for one or more participation slots, manage own participations, change up
  to one week before
- Receive messages from the grocery through the platform

## Grocer mode (staffing a distribution)

- Receive producer orders (weight/volume column, reception control and correction)
- Collect a producer order
- Distribute to members: orders, pre-orders, in-store orders; alert when an order was changed
  during distribution; simplified validation
- Take payment or top-up at the moment of distribution
- Distribution lists: pre-orders to distribute, in-store orders to distribute, "today's
  pre-orders" filter
- Available surplus of fresh products (combined view of grocery stock and supplier stock)
- Inventory (counting, adjustment)

## Admin — suppliers and products

- Create and configure suppliers and products
- Wholesaler list to fill gaps in the local offer
- Archive a product or a supplier
- Unified stock management, stock-flow view per product, weighted average cost price
- Supplier portal generated automatically
- Producer collection planning
- Food hygiene good-practice guide

## Admin — ordering and billing

- Order products from suppliers (emails with CSV attached), "cannot order a product"
  troubleshooting
- Group order between cooperatives **(multi-site)**
- POST order / consignment sale (dépôt-vente)
- FRET transport mode (paid transport, also on stock-managed products)
- Deposit products (consigne)
- Supplier invoices and credit notes, payment delay for transfers, invoice disassociation

## Admin — members and association

- Member validation and status, opening of memberships
- Variable membership fee, "pass-through" membership sub-category
- Member termination (including self-termination)
- Automatic participation exemption at age 70, generation of participation balances
- Participation planning management
- Treasury: check member top-ups, open a profit-and-loss account, association expenses
- Data exports
- Referent / IT-referent access, correction of member orders
- Communication with future members and with members through the platform
- Home page / photos / contact settings

## Extra modules

- **Announcements**: browse and search, create or edit an announcement, register local
  professionals, create and manage a participative work project
- **Services / hour exchange**: transfer participation hours to another member
- **BAR module** (beta, on request): a self-contained participative bar with its own planning,
  stock and cash register; products flagged for the bar; dedicated member account; simplified
  checkout; balance top-up and cash handling by the bar host
- Group order between cooperatives **(multi-site)**

## Support

- Support tickets (help, bug report, feature request), reopenable discussion threads
- Change history, FAQ, free training
