---
title: 'ADR 0001 — HelloAsso for online top-up, refunds only recorded'
description: Why online top-ups go through HelloAsso and why a refund is recorded, not sent to the card
---

**Status:** accepted, 2026-11-23.

## Decision

Members top up their account online through HelloAsso. The application credits a member only
after it has asked HelloAsso for the result of the payment. A refund is only recorded in the
ledger. The application never asks HelloAsso to refund a card.

## Why

HelloAsso takes no commission on payments: it lives on voluntary contributions from the payer.
That suits a cooperative where most top-ups are small. It has a sandbox, so the integration can
be tested without real money.

Its refund endpoint is not reliable for partial refunds. The API reference lists an amount
field, while HelloAsso's own rights page says a partial refund is not possible. A member who
topped up 50 € and spent 20 € could not get 30 € back on their card. Recording the refund and
letting staff give the money back by hand works in every case.

## Considered options

- **Stripe.** No legal status needed and a good test mode, and the API already reads a raw
  Stripe webhook body in `main.ts`. Rejected because of its per-payment fee on small top-ups.
- **A provider-neutral build with only a fake provider.** Rejected because nothing could be
  used in real life until a provider was chosen.
- **Refunds through HelloAsso.** Rejected for the reason above.

## Consequences

- HelloAsso requires the cooperative to be a verified association on their platform.
- The payer can add a voluntary contribution. The application credits the requested amount,
  never the total paid.
- Changing provider later means a new implementation of the payment provider seam. The ledger
  does not change.
