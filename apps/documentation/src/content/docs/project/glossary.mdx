Words the project uses with one precise meaning. Add a word here when a task introduces one.

## Wallet

- **Account** — a member's money held by the cooperative. Its balance is always the sum of its
  movements and is never stored.
- **Movement** — one row of the ledger. Written once, never edited or deleted. A mistake is
  fixed by writing another row.
- **Online top-up** — a member adds money to their account by paying through HelloAsso. The
  account is credited only after the application has asked HelloAsso for the result.
- **Manual payment** — cash, cheque or transfer received by the cooperative and typed in by a
  staff member.
- **Payment correction** — a new movement that reverses a manual payment typed wrongly. The
  original stays in the history.
- **Refund** — a negative movement that records money an admin gave back to a member by hand.
  Admin only, never larger than the balance, with a method and a reason. It is only recorded; the
  application never sends the money. There is no undo.
- **Underpaid top-up** — an online top-up where the payer paid less than they asked for. Nothing
  is credited, the top-up stays pending, and the admins get one e-mail.
- **Negative balance** — an account below zero, possible after a payment correction. The member
  cannot buy until they top up or a new payment brings the sum back to zero or above. Exactly
  zero is not blocked.

## Participation

- **Slot** — a stretch of time the cooperative needs people for: a day, a start and an end, a role
  label and a number of places.
- **Sign-up** — one member holding one place on one slot. A member has at most one sign-up per
  slot, and never two on slots that overlap.
- **Places** — how many people a slot takes. A slot with no place left is full.
- **Leave deadline** — 7 days before the slot starts. A member can leave until then. An admin can
  remove a member at any time before the slot ends.
- **Cancelled slot** — a slot an admin called off. It shows no places and takes no sign-up. It is
  final: it never comes back. Its members still see it, marked cancelled.
