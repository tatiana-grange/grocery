Delivery lot 7 from the [architecture plan](/grocery/project/architecture-plan), the last one.
The task files of this lot live in `project-management/archive/20261124-1558-lot-7-accounting-and-exports.*`.

The treasurer and the board want to know whether the grocery makes or loses money. The
accountant wants the raw figures. Everything in this lot is for admins only.

## What the lot gives

- **Association expenses.** An admin records what the cooperative pays outside its purchases:
  rent, insurance, utilities, equipment, bank fees, other. Each expense has a day, an amount, a
  category, a label and how it was paid (cash, cheque, transfer, card).
- **A profit-and-loss account** for a period the admin chooses. It opens on the current calendar
  year.
- **Five CSV files** for the same period: wallet movements, sales, purchases, membership fee
  payments and association expenses.

An admin opens **Accounting** in the back office. The first page shows the profit-and-loss
account and the five download buttons. A link leads to the expenses page, where expenses are
recorded and cancelled.

## The profit-and-loss account

| Line                   | What it adds up                                                                                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sales                  | The line totals of every handover in the period. A reversed handover counts as a negative line.     |
| Cost of goods sold     | The cost written on the stock movements of those handovers. Deliveries from suppliers do not count. |
| Gross margin           | Sales minus cost of goods sold.                                                                     |
| Membership fees        | Fee payments and fee adjustments paid in the period.                                                |
| Expenses, per category | Association expenses dated in the period, cancellations included.                                   |
| Result                 | Sales plus fees, minus cost of goods sold, minus expenses.                                          |

Why cost of goods sold and not purchases: what was bought this month may be sold next month. The
cost of what was actually sold says whether selling makes money.

Why every sale is there: the distribution table records every sale as a handover, express sales
included. The online shop charges nothing at checkout; an online order is paid when it is handed
over.

**Wallet money is not on the account.** A top-up, a payment received, a payment correction or a
refund is the members' money, held by the cooperative. It appears only in the wallet movements
export.

**A product sold before any delivery costs 0.** Its stock movement has no average cost to use
(lot 4 rule), so its cost of goods sold is 0 and the margin looks higher than it is.

No VAT: the app records no VAT rate, so every figure is as charged.

## The period

A period is a start day and an end day, both included. The days are read in the cooperative's
time zone, Europe/Paris, so a day starts at midnight Paris time in summer and in winter. A start
after the end is refused.

Each record falls in the period by its own date:

- a sale and its cost of goods: the time of the handover. Its lines and stock movements are
  written a few milliseconds later, and using their own times could split one sale across two
  periods near midnight.
- a membership fee payment: the day it was paid.
- an expense: its day.
- a wallet movement: the time it was written.
- a purchase: the time the delivery was received.

## Expenses are never edited or deleted

The expense ledger is append-only, like the wallet. A mistake is fixed by **cancelling** the
expense: the admin gives a reason, and the app writes a second row with the opposite amount that
names the expense it cancels.

- The cancellation is dated the day it was made, in Paris time. A profit-and-loss account already
  given for a past period never changes afterwards.
- An expense can be cancelled once. A cancellation cannot itself be cancelled. Cancelling is
  final: an admin who cancelled by mistake records the expense again.
- A refused cancellation answers 409 with a code: `already_cancelled` or
  `cannot_cancel_cancellation`.

## The CSV files

- Comma between values, a decimal point, a header row, UTF-8, ISO dates.
- Amounts in euros with two decimals. The API and the database keep cents.
- A value is quoted when it holds a comma, a quote or a line break. A quote inside is doubled.
- **Formula guard.** A text value that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return
  gets a single quote in front, so a spreadsheet shows it instead of running it as a formula.
  Amounts are numbers and are never changed.
- Every row has a `date` column, the Paris day that put it in the period. Rows with a time also
  have a `recordedAt` column, the exact instant in UTC.
- The sales file has the cost of goods of each line. Its totals match the account's sales and
  cost of goods sold. The fee and expense files match their lines on the account too.
- The file name says what and when: `grocery-sales-2026-01-01-2026-12-31.csv`.

This is standard CSV. A French spreadsheet set to semicolons and decimal commas needs the import
dialog.

## Not in lot 7

- VAT, a balance sheet, a stock valuation, a bank reconciliation, the French FEC file.
- A breakdown by product category or by month.
- Editable expense categories, receipts attached to an expense, recurring expenses.
- A French-Excel CSV flavour (semicolons, decimal comma).
