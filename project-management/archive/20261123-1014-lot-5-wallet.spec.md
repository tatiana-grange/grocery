**Status:** done (human sandbox check still open)

## Context

Lot 4 turned the member account into an append-only ledger and let staff record cash, cheque
and bank-transfer payments by hand. Three things are still missing. Members cannot top up
their own account. Staff cannot undo a payment typed wrongly. Nobody can record money given
back to a member.

Lot 5 adds those three. It is the fifth delivery lot of the plan in
`apps/documentation/src/content/docs/project/architecture-plan.mdx` (section "Delivery
lots"). The "Not in lot 4" section of
`apps/documentation/src/content/docs/project/lot-4-distribution.mdx` names the known gap
about mistyped payments.

## Scope

**Included:**

- A member tops up their own account online, paid through HelloAsso.
- Staff correct a payment they recorded by hand.
- A negative balance is allowed after a correction. It blocks every purchase, and an e-mail
  warns the admins and the member.
- An admin records a refund.

**Not Included:**

- Paying the membership fee online.
- Refunding a card through the HelloAsso API. A refund is only recorded.
- Correcting an online top-up.
- Reconciling against a bank feed. Manual payments stay typed by a human.
- Printed or e-mailed receipts, offline operation, collection by proxy.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **ledger-stays-append-only** — no code path updates or deletes a wallet entry. Every

- [x] **balance-is-the-sum** — the balance is always the sum of the entries and is never stored
      on the member.
- [x] **negative-balance-blocks-purchase** — a member with a negative balance cannot buy at the
      counter or online, and can still top up.

## Seams

- **Payment provider** — create a payment request for an amount and get back an address to pay
  at; later, ask for the result of that request. Observable: a fake provider drives every
  test, HelloAsso is only the production implementation.
- **Wallet ledger** — credit or charge a member inside the caller's transaction.
  Observable: the balance and the list of movements.
- **Member routes** — start a top-up, read its result. Observable over HTTP.
- **Provider notification route** — HelloAsso calls it when a payment changes. Observable:
  a repeated notification credits once, and a false claim credits nothing.
- **Staff routes** — correct a payment (any staff), record a refund (admin only).
- **Purchase guard** — the one place that refuses a purchase for a negative balance, used by
  the counter and by the online shop.
- **Member notice** — send an e-mail to the admins and to the member. Observable: messages
  sent, and a failure to send never undoes the ledger write.

## Architecture

**System design:** a new `payments` module (already in the plan's module list) owns the
payment provider seam and a record of each online top-up with its state: pending, paid,
failed or expired. The `wallet` module keeps the ledger. A top-up becomes a ledger credit in
the same transaction that marks it paid, and a unique provider payment id makes a second
credit impossible. Correction and refund are new ledger reasons in `wallet`. The purchase
guard lives with the wallet and is called by `distribution` and `orders`. E-mails go through
the existing `email` module.

## Implementation Decisions

Each one was decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-23.

- **Provider: HelloAsso.** The reasons and the rejected alternatives are in the ADR
  `apps/documentation/src/content/docs/project/decisions/0001-helloasso-and-recorded-refunds.mdx`.
- **Amount:** free, from 5 € to 200 €, in one top-up. Quick buttons (10, 20, 50 €) fill the
  field and the member can still type any amount.
- **What is credited:** the requested amount, never the total paid. HelloAsso adds an optional
  voluntary contribution that the payer can keep or remove.
- **Trust:** the balance rises only after the application has asked HelloAsso for the result of
  the payment. A notification alone is never enough.
- **Abandoned, failed or expired payment:** no ledger entry. The member can start again.
- **Who may top up:** active members only. A member with a negative balance may top up.
- **Manual payment modes stay:** cash, cheque and transfer, typed by staff.
- **Correction:** any staff member, on a payment recorded by hand, by writing a reversing
  entry. It is allowed even when the balance becomes negative.
- **Negative balance:** blocks purchases at the counter and in the online shop. An e-mail goes
  to every admin and to the member.
- **Refund:** an admin only, recorded only, reason required, never more than the balance.
- **Without HelloAsso settings:** the app still starts. The top-up button is hidden and the
  routes refuse with an explicit code. This follows the rule that a new environment variable
  is documented and optional.
- **Existing "never negative" statements** in the lot 4 contracts and comments are wrong from
  this lot on. Each one is updated in slice 02.

## Notes

Partial refunds through the HelloAsso API are unreliable: the reference lists an amount field
but HelloAsso's own rights page says they are not possible. That is why refunds are only
recorded.

## Retrospective

Added at the close, 2026-11-23.
